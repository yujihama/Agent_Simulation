## Review Result

The BC20 synthesis preserves the frozen claim boundary.

It makes bounded claims about committed artificial-organization artifacts, mechanically valid evidence packs, reviewed representative evidence, construct-validity limitations, descriptive stress-test observations, one provider-randomness sensitivity axis, and one second-domain transfer pilot.

After the Method B+ endpoint integration, it also makes bounded artificial-system claims about downstream accounting gap preservation and narrow buyer-side SL2 handoff under targeted artificial protocols.

It does not make external claims about humans, real organizations, causality, prediction, compliance, legal sufficiency, audit sufficiency, operational sufficiency, statistical significance, cross-domain validation, or general LLM behavior.

- EXP-0004 tests provider-randomness sensitivity only.
- EXP-0005 is one adjacent-domain pilot and has no human review.
- Method B+ endpoint findings remain Codex-delegated/proxy-reviewed artificial evidence unless separately reviewed by the project owner or external reviewers.

- Additional autonomous Method B+ execution is stopped unless a new mechanism-selection PR justifies a substantially different organizational mechanism before protocol freeze.
- Stable or conservative paths are treated as findings, not as failures to report.
