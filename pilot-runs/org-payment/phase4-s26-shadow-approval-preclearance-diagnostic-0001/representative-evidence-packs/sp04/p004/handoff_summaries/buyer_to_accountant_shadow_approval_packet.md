# Buyer To Accountant Shadow Approval Packet

Buyer sends the current invoice, formal approval policy excerpt, informal preclearance excerpt, and approval-preclearance context to accounting under `SP04_FORMAL_APPROVAL_PRESENT_CONTROL`.

Current approval present: `True`
Explicit approval recorded: `True`
Informal preclearance present: `True`
Approval-preclearance conflict: `False`

The packet does not ask accounting to bypass controls. It asks accounting to review whether the current invoice can proceed or whether evidence is missing.
