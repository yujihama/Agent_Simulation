# Research Objective Reframing v0.1

Date: 2026-05-17
Status: accepted
Phase: Phase 1
Checkpoint: BC1-1 research objective reframing
Claim boundary: `research_objective_reframing_only`

## Scope

This document reframes the research objective after the current evidence base, including EXP-0001 through EXP-0005, Method B, and Method B+ through the endpoint claim-hardening review.

It adds no new runs, no new scenarios, no new protocols, no new candidate reviews, no new metrics, and no stronger empirical claim. It changes only the project-level research positioning.

## Why Reframe

The early project language sometimes pointed toward a strong goal: using LLM agents to pseudo-reproduce social chaos or institutional failure. The committed evidence does not support that claim directly.

The evidence does support a narrower and more useful research objective:

- the repository can generate mechanically valid artificial-organization traces;
- Game Master decisions keep actor proposals separate from institutional outcomes;
- evidence packs and validators support reconstruction;
- candidate review and claim boundaries prevent generated labels from becoming unsupported findings;
- Method B+ separated buyer handoff, accounting preparation, final readiness, gap preservation, and gap erasure;
- current Method B+ results show repeated downstream gap preservation and narrow buyer-side SL2 handoff under limited artificial conditions.

The reframed objective treats conservative and negative results as research evidence rather than failed attempts to force a stronger failure mode.

## Old Objective Pressure

The project should not be framed as:

> Reproduce human social chaos or prove institutional failure using LLM agents.

That objective is too strong for the evidence because:

- LLM agents are artificial participants, not human substitutes;
- the scenarios are constrained artificial organizations;
- most reviewed findings are bounded artifact, observation, or construct-limited claims;
- many stronger failure-mode candidates were rejected or not observed;
- no statistical, causal, real-world, compliance, legal, audit, or operational sufficiency claim is supported.

## Reframed Objective

The project objective is:

> Build and evaluate a reviewable artificial-organization research method that uses LLM-controlled roles, deterministic Game Master boundaries, evidence packs, validation, and candidate review to study how institutional friction and non-intentional control slippage can be represented, separated, and bounded in artificial settings.

This objective emphasizes method and evidence structure over social reproduction.

## Core Research Object

The research object is not "human society." It is a controlled artificial organization that can produce traceable artifacts about:

- institutional friction;
- approval and evidence gaps;
- pressure context;
- coordination holds;
- buyer-side handoff without explicit approval;
- downstream boundary preservation;
- generated candidate false positives;
- claim-boundary discipline.

The Method B+ endpoint is central to the reframing. It shows why stage separation matters:

- SL2 buyer-side handoff can appear under bounded artificial conditions, especially lossy handoff.
- SL5 downstream evidence-gap preservation repeatedly appears in S17, S18, and S19.
- SL3 accountant payment preparation without explicit approval is not supported.
- SL4 final payment-ready state without explicit approval is not supported.
- SL6 evidence-gap erasure is not supported.
- FM1 responsibility diffusion, FM3 ambiguous-guidance misinterpretation, and FM6 post-hoc justification remain unsupported, reviewed rejected, or not observed.

## What The Research Can Say

The project can claim that:

- it has built a staged artificial-organization research pipeline;
- the pipeline can generate, validate, review, and synthesize traceable artificial interactions;
- current evidence supports bounded observations about evidence gaps, coordination holds, pressure context, and downstream boundary preservation;
- Method B+ supports narrow buyer-side SL2 handoff under limited artificial conditions and repeated downstream SL5 preservation;
- the artifacts can generate hypotheses for future study.

## What The Research Cannot Say

The project cannot claim that:

- LLM agents reproduce human society;
- human organizations would behave like these artificial runs;
- real organizations can be predicted from the artifacts;
- full approval bypass was reproduced;
- responsibility diffusion, evidence-gap erasure, ambiguous-guidance misinterpretation, or post-hoc justification was proven;
- Method B+ supports a controlled failure-mode baseline;
- any result is statistically significant;
- any prompt, scenario, menu, or model caused an observed behavior;
- the system provides compliance, legal, audit, operational, governance, safety, or model-reliability sufficiency.

## Research Position

The project is best positioned as a methodology and artifact-validation study for artificial institutional-friction simulation.

It is not yet an empirical study of human organizations. It is not a proof that any failure mode is present or absent in the real world. Its value is that it creates a disciplined way to generate, reconstruct, review, reject, and bound artificial evidence before stronger research claims are attempted.
