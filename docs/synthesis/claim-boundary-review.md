## Review Result

The BC20 synthesis preserves the frozen claim boundary.

It makes bounded claims about committed artificial-organization artifacts, mechanically valid evidence packs, reviewed representative evidence, construct-validity limitations, descriptive stress-test observations, one provider-randomness sensitivity axis, and one second-domain transfer pilot.

It does not make external claims about humans, real organizations, causality, prediction, compliance, legal sufficiency, audit sufficiency, operational sufficiency, statistical significance, cross-domain validation, or general LLM behavior.
