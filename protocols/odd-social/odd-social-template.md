# ODD-Social Template

Date: 2026-05-14
Status: accepted
Phase: P1
Step: P1/P2 design bundle
Covers: C04
Supersedes: none
Related ADR: ADR-0001, ADR-0002, ADR-0003

Use this template when adding a new artificial organization or scenario family.

## 1. Overview

### Purpose

State what institutional friction or social mechanism this model is meant to explore.

### Research Positioning

Explain why this is a hypothesis-generation or stress-test model, not a direct prediction of real human behavior.

### Domain Boundary

List what exists in the artificial organization and what is explicitly excluded.

### Entities

List roles, documents, cases, communication channels, records, and institutional artifacts.

### State Variables

List state variables, who can observe them, who can propose changes, and how the Game Master decides final state.

### Process Overview

Describe one run from initialization to termination.

### Scheduling Assumptions

Describe turn order, intervention points, and whether actions are synchronous or asynchronous.

### Termination Condition

Define when the run ends.

## 2. Design Concepts

### Emergence

Describe what higher-level failure pattern may emerge from local action.

### Adaptation

Describe how agents may adapt to pressure, policy, or prior outcomes.

### Objectives

Separate public obligations from private incentives.

### Learning Or Memory

Describe whether agents remember prior actions, policies, or exceptions.

### Sensing

Describe what each role can observe.

### Interaction

Describe official and informal interaction paths.

### Stochasticity

Describe randomness or variation sources, if any.

### Collectives

Describe teams, hierarchy, departments, or external parties.

### Observation

Describe what the model should preserve for later review without defining metrics.

## 3. Details

### Initialization

List initial case data, documents, role assignments, and pressure conditions.

### Input Artifacts

List policy documents, invoices, message histories, or records used by the model.

### Submodels

List any submodels such as approval handling, vendor pressure, audit intervention, or memory.

### Randomness Policy

Describe whether seeds, model choices, or scenario variants are controlled.

### Run Boundary

Define what one run includes and excludes.

### Non-Goals

List items explicitly out of scope for this model.

## 4. Institution Extension

### Formal Rules

List written rules.

### Informal Norms

List unwritten norms.

### Authority Model

List who can approve, process, reject, escalate, or audit.

### Segregation Of Duties

Describe role separation requirements.

### Exception Handling

Describe how exceptions are requested, approved, or rejected.

### Auditability

Describe what must be recorded.

### Sanctions

Describe simulated consequences or warnings.

### Control Mode

Specify soft, monitored, or hard control.

## 5. Social Structure Extension

### Role Assignment

List participant roles.

### Hierarchy

Describe reporting relationships.

### Dependency Relation

Describe operational dependencies.

### Trust Network

Describe trust or reliance assumptions.

### Information Asymmetry

Describe who lacks important information.

### Communication Channels

List official and informal channels.

### Private Goals

Describe role incentives or pressures.

### Public Obligations

Describe expected duties.

## 6. Chaos Factors Extension

### Policy Ambiguity

Clear, ambiguous, or contradictory.

### Deadline Pressure

Low, medium, or high.

### External Pressure

None, vendor, customer, superior, or other.

### Workload

Low, medium, or high.

### Role Overlap

Separated, partial, or duplicated.

### Blame Avoidance

Low, medium, or high.

### Informal Norm Strength

Weak, moderate, or strong.

### Memory Persistence

None, short, or long.
